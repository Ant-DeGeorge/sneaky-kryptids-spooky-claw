How it works

Slide the claw. Drop it on the glowing prize. Fill your five pockets before Redcap fills his five caps.

🟢 Aim for the green glow. A clean grab fills a pocket. A miss and Redcap swipes one.

⭐ Golden claw comes around now and then: easy grab, steals one back, or takes two.

😈 Redcap sneaks in. Tap A fast to bonk him and earn a bonus star.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Rank every level. Stars = 5 − caps Redcap filled + sneaks you bonked.

💪 Lose and try again. Each loss makes the next try a little easier.

Put it on a real cartridge

Made for the ModRetro Chromatic with a Tetris cartridge and ChroMagician.

  1. On the handheld: SYSTEM → C. MAGICIAN = ON.
  2. In ChroMagician, back up the Tetris cart first. Writing homebrew replaces it.
  3. Write Homebrew and pick spooky_claw3.gbc. Wait for write and verify.
  4. C. MAGICIAN = OFF, then power-cycle.
  5. No boot? Write spooky_claw3_nosave.gbc instead.

Progress saves after every star screen when the cartridge has save memory. Hold B and press A on the title for a new game.

Build it yourself

git clone https://github.com/Ant-DeGeorge/sneaky-kryptids-spooky-claw
cd sneaky-kryptids-spooky-claw
make GBDK_HOME=/path/to/gbdk      # Windows: build.bat (GBDK at C:\gbdk)

Written in C with GBDK-2020. Art is ASCII pixel grids in tools/art.py, music is note strings in tools/gen_sound.py.
